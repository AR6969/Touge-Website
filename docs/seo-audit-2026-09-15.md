# SEO audit — September 15, 2026

## Scope and selection

Updated the homepage, `/roads`, `/drives`, and ten individual road pages. The six homepage-featured roads were prioritized, plus Pescadero Creek Road, Coastside Highway 1, Glendora Mountain Road and western Angeles Crest for their distinctive routes and guide connections. This is an editorial selection, **not a ranking from private Search Console data**; that account's performance and indexing reports were not available in this session.

The homepage remains map-first. No map behavior, filters, road geometry, measurements, speed limits, scores, regional navigation or video playback implementation was changed. Editorial notes live in `app/lib/road-guides.ts`, separate from the generated road catalog.

## Findings

- All 13 live target URLs returned HTTP 200 with indexable robots metadata and correct self-referencing `https://www.tougemap.com` canonicals. HTTP→HTTPS, apex→www, trailing-slash removal and `/bay-area`→`/` redirects returned permanent HTTP 308 responses. There was no need to change their URLs or canonical policy.
- Road titles carried long area names and repeated “Driving Road” boilerplate. Descriptions were dominated by bend counts, grades and difficulty, giving little reason to choose a route.
- The directory already linked to every road in server-rendered HTML. Its ItemList described the comparison table's curvature order rather than the main visible regional directory; it now follows the directory.
- Road pages already had regional breadcrumbs, nearby-road links, maps and measurements. New contextual connections explain which roads actually fit the outing; proximity alone does not establish a useful connection.
- Phone layouts had no horizontal overflow in the baseline, but several directory and breadcrumb links were small tap targets.

## Research

Editorial practices checked against [Google's title guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link), [crawlable internal links and anchor text](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable), and [Schema.org CollectionPage](https://schema.org/CollectionPage). Metadata and sitemap implementation checked against the installed Next.js 16.3.4 documentation.

Road facts use official sources, with each link placed beside the relevant paragraph on the road page:

- [Midpen Saratoga Gap](https://www.openspace.org/preserves/saratoga-gap): Highway 9/35 junction.
- [Midpen Russian Ridge](https://www.openspace.org/preserves/russian-ridge): Skyline/Page Mill/Alpine junction and preserve access.
- [Alice's Restaurant](https://alicesrestaurant.com/): visitor location.
- [San Mateo County Memorial Park](https://www.smcgov.org/parks/memorial-park): redwood stop on Pescadero Creek Road.
- [Pescadero State Beach](https://parks.ca.gov/?page_id=522): Highway 1/Pescadero Creek Road access.
- [Santa Clara County Mines Road repair location](https://roads.santaclaracounty.gov/services/storm-damaged-roads/mines-road-site-1): southern junction reference, not a claim of present road condition.
- [LA County closures](https://pw.lacounty.gov/roadclosures/): official access-check destination. The web tool's cached report was stale, so it was **not** treated as proof that GMR or Latigo is open today.
- [Caltrans SR 2 conditions](https://roads.dot.ca.gov/?roadnumber=2): the September 15 report still listed eastern closure segments. New copy links the live report rather than hardcoding a reopening claim.
- [LA County Latigo trailhead](https://trails.lacounty.gov/Trail/74/backbone-trail---latigo-canyon): Backbone Trail access along Latigo.
- [Forest Service project report](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sopa/components/reports/sopa-110502-2024-07.pdf): Palomar S6/S7/Summit junction; used for the stable location only.
- [Caltech Palomar visitor information](https://sites.astro.caltech.edu/palomar/visitor/): separate observatory access planning.

No new road geometry, speed limit, travel time or safety rating was inferred from these sources.

## Validation

- ESLint and production webpack build passed with the current lockfile dependencies; all 333 static outputs generated. Validation used an isolated `/tmp` checkout to avoid iCloud-offloaded dependency files in the workspace.
- `python3 scripts/check-seo.py http://127.0.0.1:3102` checks the actual production HTML for unique titles/descriptions, matching Open Graph and Twitter titles, one H1, canonical URLs, robots, viewport metadata and valid JSON-LD types.
- The crawl checks 199 internal destinations/section anchors, all 182 sitemap URL destinations, editorial last-modified dates, missing-road HTTP 404/noindex, and the clean homepage canonical with region/tracking parameters.
- Phone checks cover all 13 pages at 390px and 320px. No horizontal overflow; discovery, breadcrumb, shortcut and connection targets checked. Visual inspection includes the homepage map, road directory, driving guide directory and planning notes.
- GMR's MP4 is not requested on initial page load.

## Limits and follow-up

These checks establish technical crawlability, not Google's chosen canonical, actual indexing or future ranking. Search Console's private reports and real-user Core Web Vitals were not available. Browser checks emulate narrow phones; they do not replace testing on a physical iPhone.

After deployment, inspect the homepage, `/roads`, `/drives` and GMR in Search Console. Compare impressions, clicks and queries for these 13 pages over the next few weeks, and use actual performance to choose the next editorial batch. Google may rewrite titles or descriptions and must recrawl to see changes.
